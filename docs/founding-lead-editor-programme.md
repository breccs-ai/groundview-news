# Founding Lead Editor programme

## Commercial boundary

Ground View News retains 70% of net advertising revenue. The writer/editor pool remains capped at 30%. An accepted Founding Lead Editor receives a 1.02 weighting on earnings attributable to their own qualifying work inside that pool; this is not an additional two percentage points of total revenue and it cannot reduce the platform share to 68%.

Earnings remain dependent on collected advertising revenue and meaningful reader engagement. They are not guaranteed income, employment, or a fixed salary.

## Qualification and capacity

- A journalist qualifies after five distinct, legitimate articles reach `published` status following editorial review.
- Test, duplicate, deleted, or administratively disqualified articles do not count.
- Up to ten places may be reserved or accepted at once.
- An invitation reserves a place for 14 days.
- Accepted places remain occupied until revoked or relinquished.
- Eligible journalists are waitlisted when all places are reserved or accepted.
- A database transaction lock prevents an eleventh simultaneous reservation.
- A journalist receives only one founding offer; publishing retries cannot duplicate or restore it.

## Staged rollout

1. Programme state, cap, audit records, communication preferences and feedback records.
2. Detect the fifth qualifying publication and send the invitation or waiting-list notice.
3. Add dashboard acceptance, decline and structured feedback controls.
4. Add restricted editorial permissions, conflict protection and action auditing.
5. Apply the 1.02 engagement weighting inside the unchanged 30% pool.
6. Add quality-gated journalist referrals and collected-revenue advertiser introductions.

Stage 1 creates no editorial permissions and changes no earnings.

## Journalist communications

Material changes should be explained in plain language, including what changed, why it matters, when it takes effect, whether action is required, and where to ask questions. Communications must not disclose private financial or security information, promise earnings, imply employment, compromise editorial independence, or pressure journalists to recruit, market, or introduce advertisers.

Optional updates and feedback invitations respect communication preferences. Essential account, article-decision, security and payment messages remain transactional.

Feedback prompts should ask:

- What subjects and editorial tasks do you feel confident handling?
- What training, tools or feedback would help you produce stronger work?
- What would make the dashboard and publishing process more useful?
- Are you comfortable mentoring or reviewing other contributors?
- Do you have ideas for reaching genuine readers or suitable advertising partners?

Suggestions are advisory. Editorial standards, access control, privacy, revenue limits and final business decisions remain with Ground View News.
